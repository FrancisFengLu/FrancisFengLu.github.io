title: "cuSAIGE: GPU-Accelerated Genome-Wide Association Testing at Biobank Scale"
excerpt: "cuSAIGE is a GPU implementation of SAIGE. To our knowledge, it is the first to run both steps of SAIGE's mixed-model genome-wide association analysis entirely on the GPU, and the only GPU method that supports mixed-model association testing of binary traits. It runs tens of thousands of times faster than the original SAIGE (up to ~59,000&times;) and cuts the cost of binary-trait analyses by more than two orders of magnitude: in All of Us, 1,024 binary traits cost about US$1.8 instead of US$1,530.
