title: "cuSAIGE: GPU-Accelerated Genome-Wide Association Testing at Biobank Scale"
excerpt: "cuSAIGE is a GPU implementation of SAIGE. It is the first method to demonstrate that GPUs lower the overall cost of genome-wide association analysis (50&times; to 4,500&times;), while remaining mathematically identical to SAIGE, with results differing only in the last digits. It runs about 3,400&times; faster than the original SAIGE for binary traits and 15,000&times; faster for quantitative traits, and substantially faster than all competing methods. It also solves the problem of running binary-trait corrections on the GPU.
